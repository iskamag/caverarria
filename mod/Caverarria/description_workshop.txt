[h1]Cave Story, inside Terraria[/h1]
Bring your Terraria character into the original Cave Story freeware campaign. The Rust doukutsu-rs engine runs its maps, dialogue, enemies, bosses, story and saves. Terraria runs your character, equipment and combat.

[h2]Play[/h2]
Enable Caverarria, then create a single-player world with seed [b]caverarria[/b]. Enter it with your character. Each campaign world keeps its own story progress.

The first entry downloads and verifies the original English freeware data. The portable engine is included in the mod. Original Organya music and PixTone sound effects play through Terraria's audio system.

Use Terraria movement and attacks. [b]E[/b]: interact and advance dialogue. [b]I[/b]: story inventory. [b]M[/b]: story map. [b]V/C[/b]: cycle story weapons. [b]R[/b]: retry from the campaign save. Original save points work; [b]/caverarria save[/b] also saves your progress.

[h2]Playable alpha 0.1.0[/h2]
Single player. Linux client tested, including 60 Hz movement, original audio looping and a normal Save & Exit. Full campaign completion and Windows/macOS playthroughs remain under verification. Authored terrain is protected; world editing is planned.
