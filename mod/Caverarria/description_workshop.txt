[h1]Cave Story, inside Terraria[/h1]
The Rust doukutsu-rs engine (compiled to sandboxed WASM) runs its maps, dialogue, enemies, bosses, story and saves. Terraria runs your character, equipment and combat.

[h2]Play[/h2]
Enable Caverarria, then create a single-player world with seed [b]caverarria[/b]. Enter it with your character. Each campaign world keeps its own story progress.

The first entry downloads and verifies the original English freeware data.

Use Terraria movement and attacks. [b]E[/b]: interact and advance dialogue. [b]I[/b]: Terraria inventory (right-click story items). [b]M[/b]: story map. [b]V/C[/b]: cycle story weapons. [b]R[/b]: retry from the campaign save. Hold [b]X[/b] to skip cutscenes. New mod keys may be Unbound: assign them or use Reset to Default under Settings → Controls → Mod Controls → Caverarria (Alpha).

Original save points work; [b]/caverarria save[/b] also saves your progress.
