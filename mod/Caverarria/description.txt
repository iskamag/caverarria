Playable alpha: bring your real Terraria character into the original Cave Story freeware campaign.
The original doukutsu-rs engine runs the story, enemies, and encounters. Terraria runs your character, equipment, and combat.
Create a world with seed caverarria, then enter it from the normal world selector with any character. Each campaign world keeps its own progress. The portable engine and managed WASM runner are embedded in the mod. Original freeware data is downloaded and verified separately on first entry, under the tModLoader save folder's Caverarria directory.
Mine campaign terrain with pickaxes and build with ordinary solid blocks. Terrain persistence and Terraria-sized block placement are optional in Mod Configuration. Avatar collision matches its body size, with host world actors rendered on the campaign pixel grid. Ordinary non-solid furniture, including Slice of Cake, can be placed and used. Containers, tile entities, falling blocks, platforms and explosives are not supported yet.
Single player. Tested on Linux; full campaign completion and Windows/macOS playthroughs are still being verified. Report the room, what happened, and your platform/mod list at https://github.com/iskamag/caverarria/issues.
